FILE 01 / A SHORT INTRODUCTION

Hello, I’m Tarun.

I make useful things for people, and try to make them feel a little more human.

I’m an independent designer and writer working at the intersection of clear ideas and thoughtful interfaces. Over the last several years I’ve helped small teams turn complicated things into approachable ones.

My process starts with listening, a notebook full of questions, and a slightly unreasonable belief that the details matter. I like old computers, long walks, and websites with a point of view.

Currently available for a few good collaborations.
