FEBRUARY 02, 2025 / THE WEB

A website is a place, not a pitch

A good website feels less like a funnel and more like being welcomed into a room.

MAKE ROOM TO LOOK AROUND

The web feels different when a page seems made by someone, for someone. Not just a sequence of buttons aimed at a conversion, but a place with a particular voice, a point of view, and a little room to wander.

DETAILS SET THE TEMPERATURE

A short note in the margin. A photograph with context. A useful link that is not trying to sell anything. These small decisions tell a visitor what kind of place they are in, and whether they can take their time.

That does not mean a website should hide its purpose. The practical details should be easy to find. A welcoming room still needs a door, a light switch, and a clear way back out.

BUILD FOR A PERSON

When I start a site, I try to picture one real person arriving with one real question. If the page helps them feel oriented, curious, or understood, the rest of the experience tends to follow.
