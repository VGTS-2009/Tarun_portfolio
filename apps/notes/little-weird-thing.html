OCTOBER 19, 2024 / PROCESS

Keep the little weird thing

The detail that almost does not fit can be the part that makes the work feel alive.

THE EDGE OF THE BRIEF

Every project seems to grow one detail that does not quite belong. A strange color pairing. A phrase someone said in a meeting. A tiny interaction that is probably unnecessary, but makes everyone smile.

DON’T SAND IT DOWN TOO SOON

It is tempting to remove those edges in the name of consistency. Sometimes that is the right call. But if every unexpected thing is tidied away, the work can become correct and strangely forgettable.

I have started asking what a detail is doing before I decide whether it stays. If it brings clarity, warmth, or character, I try to protect it long enough to find out.

A SMALL PERMISSION

Not every idea has to justify itself with a metric before it gets a chance. Leave a little space for the detail that makes the project unmistakably yours.
