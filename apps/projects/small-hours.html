THE BRIEF

Create a warm, tactile identity for a small publication collecting personal rituals, morning routines, and late-night thoughts.

THE APPROACH

Typewriter notes, restrained color, and generous page margins give each contributor room to sound like themselves. The digital edition borrows the pace of flipping through a printed journal.

THE OUTCOME

A repeatable issue template, launch identity, and reading experience for a growing collection of short personal essays.
