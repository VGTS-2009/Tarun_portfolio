Soft Systems

A more human way to explore complex climate research.

FIELD
NOTES / 01
ROLEVisual design, prototyping
PARTNERSSoft Systems Lab
YEAR2024

THE BRIEF

Turn dense climate research into a clear public resource that keeps nuance intact while helping readers find the information relevant to them.

THE APPROACH

We grouped findings around real questions, paired summaries with source material, and used a calm visual language to make evidence and uncertainty easy to distinguish.

THE OUTCOME

A modular editorial system and interactive prototype designed to make long-form research easier to navigate and share.
