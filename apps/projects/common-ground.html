Common Ground

A welcoming identity and digital home for a neighborhood food co-op.

GOOD
GROWS HERE
ROLEArt direction, design
PARTNERSCommon Ground Co-op
YEAR2025

THE BRIEF

Help a member-owned food co-op explain how it works, welcome new members, and make local food easier to find. The experience needed to feel open to the whole neighborhood.

THE APPROACH

We built the identity around shared tables, handwritten market notes, and an easy-to-scan weekly produce guide. The website puts membership, hours, and seasonal updates within quick reach.

THE OUTCOME

A flexible visual system for in-store and online use, alongside a responsive site that makes the co-op’s everyday details easier to discover.
